ABANDONWARE - CAUTION

The core loop looks appealing at first glance. Then the design collapses. Too many classes. No balance. Several units are built for AI only: suicidal, expendable, either overtuned or pure annoyance.

Not talking about bosses or quest minions.

Necromancers: a unit useful only after something is already dead. Works for AI. Fails for player.

Roughly 30% of melee classes are dead weight. Stun orcs: too weak to engage, too slow to reach archers, too low damage to kill DD. To make them function you must invest in speed, HP, armor, damage, agility. Result: expensive bullet fodder.

Viable builds are limited to single-stat abuse: archers, taunt-spiked tanks, warlocks/banshees. Everything else is strictly inferior.

Invisible melee storm chasers: first hit deals extreme damage with AOE stun. Cannot be manually targeted because invisible. If positioning fails, you lose a unit before interaction. After that, the attacker becomes useless. Building this unit as player is pointless: AI outnumbers you, 1-for-1 trades are losing trades.

Even bad balance could be tolerated. The real problem: critical bugs.

* Abilities frequently not working
* Random crashes
* Inconsistent damage
* Bugged deaths

Check Steam discussions: 100+ reports. No dev response.

Devs publicly stated they are done with the game and moved to another project.

Advice: skip the game. Ignore the developer.

Early Access Review

I was so disappointed in the this game for the fact it is so heavily time gated. Every single fight you partake in with your gladiators, the game arbitrarily decides how many of said gladiators you can use. You can easily gain 8+ gladiators early on but only use a max of 3 or 5 in fewer cases in a single battle thereby limiting the amount of XP you can earn per battle. Also there is a time system where by you must wait, real time, for the next main event to occur before you can progress the story.

Time gating is a game design that should not be tolerated by anyone. Games should be enjoyed at our own pace because time is something we are all in limited supply of.

Once again, expectations got the best of me, I only paid around 3 euros for this so it isn't too bad.

It is sold as a management game; there is almost no management here. It is primarily an auto battler with focus on team composition and lite town building mechanics.

I felt nostalgic playing it, very reminiscent from the early 2000s, playing a flash game after school for the afternoon on Kongregate's website.

Sense of humor was a little bit corny and silly, totally up my alley though, and the voiceover added an unexpected (though thin) extra layer of quality which unfortunately was brought down to earth by the incredibly dumb AI. I lost some fights that I should've definitely not lost.

When it comes to content, well there isn't much substance here, nor was it expected, it rewarded me enough while keeping me engaged for the 5 hours I played. What I paid was a fair price, not sure if I would recommend paying over 10, although it rarely stays at that price point anymore.
